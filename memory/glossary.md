# Glossary

What this file is: the project’s shared list of important terms and their intended meanings. Who writes it: your AI from your language. When you read it: when a term is unclear or could have more than one meaning.

- **Experiment:** one recorded test of a hypothesis.
- **Hypothesis:** a testable statement that could be wrong.
- **Insight:** a short conclusion that remains useful after a run.
- **Frozen code:** a copy of the exact code used for one experiment.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a newer result and no longer valid as evidence.

# Researcher preferences

What this file is: the record of how you want the AI to communicate and work. Who writes it: your AI from your answers. When you read it: when you want to check or change how the AI helps you.

- Reply language: not set.
- Result detail: not set.
- Tools already used: not set.
- Other preferences: not set.

The [welcome skill](../.claude/skills/welcome/SKILL.md) fills this file when you start your project.
